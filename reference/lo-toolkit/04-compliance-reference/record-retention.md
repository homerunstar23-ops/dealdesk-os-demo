# Record retention (high level)

> **⚠️ Responsibility & approval notice.** Cory remains responsible for his licensing, sponsorship (sponsoring-broker approval of all marketing and outside business activities), RESPA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, fair lending (ECOA/FHA/UDAAP) and state rules. **Get sponsoring-broker compliance sign-off before any public or borrower-facing use.** This is educational/support material only — not legal advice, not a credit decision, and not a replacement for underwriters, compliance staff or company systems. Keep borrower data (NPI) only in company-approved systems. Fill every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` before use.

- **Reg B §1002.12** sets retention periods for application records — generally 25 months after notifying the applicant of action taken for consumer credit, and 12 months for most business credit (with a 60-day rule for certain larger-business/trade credit), plus extensions during investigations. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/12/
- **Reg Z §1026.25** sets record-retention rules for TILA-covered (consumer) transactions, including specific provisions for mortgage loans and loan originator compensation records. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/25/
- **State laws** and the sponsoring company's policy usually add requirements (often longer).
- **Marketing records:** keep copies of approved ads, approval dates, consent records (TCPA), DNC scrub logs and opt-out logs — ask compliance for the retention period.
- Store borrower records only in company-approved systems; don't keep NPI in personal drives, email or this toolkit's spreadsheets unless the sponsor approves the storage location.
