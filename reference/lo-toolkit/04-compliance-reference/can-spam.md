Rule text: 16 CFR Part 316 — https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-316

**Main requirements (high level, from the FTC's business guidance):**
1. No false or misleading header information (From, To, Reply-To, routing)
2. No deceptive subject lines
3. Identify commercial messages as advertisements (clearly and conspicuously)
4. Include a valid physical postal address
5. Tell recipients how to opt out, clearly
6. Honor opt-outs promptly — mechanism must work for at least 30 days after sending; honor within 10 business days; no fee or extra steps; don't sell/transfer the address
7. You remain responsible for vendors sending on your behalf

Covers business-to-business email too. Transactional/relationship messages (e.g., loan status updates) have narrower requirements but still can't be deceptive — ask compliance how your status emails are classified.
