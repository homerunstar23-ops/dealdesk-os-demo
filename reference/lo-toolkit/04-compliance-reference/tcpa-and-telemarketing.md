**Core concepts (high level)**
- The FCC's TCPA rules (47 CFR §64.1200) restrict telemarketing calls and texts — especially those using an autodialer or artificial/prerecorded voice — and require **prior express written consent** for many marketing calls/texts to wireless numbers. https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200
- The rules set calling-time limits for telephone solicitations (not before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. called party's local time), company-specific do-not-call list obligations, and National Do Not Call Registry restrictions.
- National DNC Registry access for telemarketers: https://telemarketing.donotcall.gov/ · FCC consumer guide: https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/telephone-consumer-protection-act
- The FCC has treated AI-generated voices as "artificial" voice under the TCPA — confirm current position with compliance.
- **States add rules.** Florida and several other states have their own telemarketing laws (often stricter on hours, call frequency, consent and autodialer definitions). Cory is in Florida — have compliance confirm Florida requirements before any calling/texting campaign.
- Consent rules for lead generators and comparison sites have been the subject of recent FCC rulemaking and litigation — **confirm current status before buying or using third-party leads.**
