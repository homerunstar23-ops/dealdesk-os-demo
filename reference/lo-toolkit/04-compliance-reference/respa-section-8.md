# RESPA Section 8 — referral fees, kickbacks, marketing services agreements

**What it covers (high level):** RESPA Section 8 and Regulation X §1024.14 prohibit giving or accepting any fee, kickback or thing of value under an agreement or understanding to refer settlement-service business involving a **federally related mortgage loan**, and prohibit splitting charges for services not actually performed. Payments for goods or facilities actually furnished or services actually performed are permitted when bona fide.
- Reg X §1024.14: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/14/
- CFPB RESPA FAQs (include MSA discussion): https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/mortgage-resources/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act/real-estate-settlement-procedures-act-faqs/

**Coverage note:** Reg X §1024.5(b)(2) exempts credit primarily for a business, commercial or agricultural purpose as defined in Reg Z §1026.3(a)(1); §1024.5(b)(3) also addresses temporary financing and bridge loans. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/5/ — **Do not assume an exemption applies to a given loan or relationship; company policy, state law and other rules may still restrict referral payments. Get compliance's written answer.**

**Marketing services agreements (MSAs):** The CFPB rescinded its 2015 MSA compliance bulletin in October 2020 and stated RESPA itself is unchanged (https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/supervisory-guidance/bulletin-respa-compliance-marketing-services-agreements/). The RESPA FAQs describe when MSA payments may be permissible (actual, necessary, distinct services at reasonable market value) and risks when payments track referrals. **Treat any MSA, co-marketing or lead-sharing arrangement as attorney + compliance review required.**

**Practical caution list**
- No gifts, cash, gift cards, paid events or "thank-you" items tied to referrals
- No paying agents/wholesalers/PMs per lead or per closed loan
- Co-marketing (shared ads, events, websites): each party pays its fair share for actual services, documented, compliance-approved
- Lead generation vendors: compliance review of pay structure (per-lead vs. per-referral distinctions matter)
- Don't accept things of value from title, appraisal, insurance or other providers for steering business
