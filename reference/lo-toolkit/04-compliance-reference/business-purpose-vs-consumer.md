- **Reg Z §1026.3(a)** exempts credit extended primarily for a business, commercial or agricultural purpose from TILA/Reg Z. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/3/ The official commentary gives factors for determining primary purpose, and includes specific guidance for credit to acquire, improve or maintain **non-owner-occupied rental property** (treatment differs depending on number of units and whether the borrower will occupy) — review the commentary with compliance.
- **Reg X §1024.5(b)(2)** exempts business-purpose loans (as defined by Reg Z §1026.3(a)(1)) from RESPA. https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1024/5/
- **ECOA/Reg B still applies** to business credit (with some different notice/retention rules). https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/
- **TCPA, CAN-SPAM, UDAAP, state licensing and state usury/lending laws can apply regardless** of business purpose.
- **State law varies:** some states require licenses for business-purpose lending/brokering on residential property; others exempt it.

**Why the determination is high-stakes**
- Misclassifying a consumer (owner-occupied or personal-use) loan as business-purpose can mean missing required disclosures, ability-to-repay rules, and more.
- Typical controls: occupancy and business-purpose certifications, use-of-proceeds statements, red-flag review for occupancy. Follow **company** procedures; don't create your own.
