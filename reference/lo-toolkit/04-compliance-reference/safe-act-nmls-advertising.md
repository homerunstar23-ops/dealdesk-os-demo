- The SAFE Act framework (CFPB Regulation H, 12 CFR Part 1008) sets minimum standards for state licensing and registration of mortgage loan originators via the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System & Registry (NMLS). https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1008/
- **Reg Z §1026.36(g):** for consumer credit secured by a dwelling, the loan originator organization's and individual originator's names and NMLSR IDs must appear on certain loan documents (credit application, note, security instrument). https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/36/
- **Advertising disclosures** (NMLS ID on ads, company name/license number, state-specific statements, "NMLS Consumer Access" reference) are driven largely by **state law** and vary by state — see NMLS State Licensing Requirements: https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/slr/Pages/default.aspx
- **Sponsorship:** a state-licensed MLO's license is generally sponsored by a licensed company; the company is typically responsible for supervising the MLO's origination activity and advertising. Marketing under a personal brand or a separate business (like DealSheet Lab) typically requires the sponsor's **written** approval of the outside business activity and pre-approval of advertising.
