# Follow-up cadences

> **⚠️ Responsibility & approval notice.** Cory remains responsible for his licensing, sponsorship (sponsoring-broker approval of all marketing and outside business activities), RESPA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, fair lending (ECOA/FHA/UDAAP) and state rules. **Get sponsoring-broker compliance sign-off before any public or borrower-facing use.** This is educational/support material only — not legal advice, not a credit decision, and not a replacement for underwriters, compliance staff or company systems. Keep borrower data (NPI) only in company-approved systems. Fill every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` before use.

**Ground rules for every cadence**
- Contact only people who gave you their info and the right **consent for the channel** (see `../02-lead-gen/consent-and-disclosure-rules.md`). Log consent (date, channel, how obtained) in the tracker's "Consent on file?" column.
- Calls/texts to cell phones using autodialers, prerecorded/AI voice, or marketing texts generally need **prior express written consent**; manual 1:1 calls still require **DNC scrubbing** for marketing. Confirm your sponsor's current policy.
- Stop immediately on any opt-out ("stop", "unsubscribe", "don't call") and record it.
- Use only company-approved systems for borrower NPI. No SSNs, DOBs or account numbers in notes, texts or personal email.

## A. New inbound lead (website tool / checklist download / referral)
| Day | Channel | Action | Template |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 (within 1 business hour if possible) | Email | Thank them; ask 3 scenario questions | F-1 |
| 1 | Call if consented, else email | Offer a 15-minute scenario call | F-2 |
| 3 | Email | Share one relevant educational tool/guide | F-3 |
| 7 | Email | "Anything under contract yet?" | F-4 |
| 14 | Email | Move to monthly educational newsletter (if opted in) | F-5 |

## B. Scenario reviewed → documents
| Day | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Email | Send loan-type checklist (`doc-collection-checklists.md`) + secure company upload link |
| 2 | Email / text (if written consent) | Reminder listing only outstanding items |
| 5 | Call | Resolve blockers; agree on target dates |
| 8 | Email | Final reminder + "should we pause the file?" |

## C. In process (submitted → clear to close)
- Weekly status update every Friday (template S-2), **plus** same-day update when anything changes (appraisal in, conditions issued, CTC).
- Keep referral partners updated **only with borrower authorization** and only with what they need.

## D. Post-close
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Thank-you + closing recap (S-6) |
| 30 | Check-in; ask if they'd be comfortable introducing another investor (no fee/gift for referrals — RESPA Section 8 caution) |
| 90 / every 6 months | "What's next?" check-in, educational content |

## E. Lost / withdrawn
Day 0 feedback request (no pressure). Day 60 check-in only if still opted in.
