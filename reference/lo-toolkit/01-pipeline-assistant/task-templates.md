## Daily (30–45 min power block)
- [ ] Open the pipeline tracker → filter **Overdue?** = OVERDUE / TODAY; clear those first
- [ ] Respond to new leads (cadence A, touch 1)
- [ ] Check processing/UW queue in company LOS; send status updates where anything changed
- [ ] 5 relationship touches to referral partners (scripts in `../02-lead-gen/`) — consented channels only
- [ ] Log every contact: date, channel, outcome, next action, next follow-up date

## Weekly
- [ ] Friday status update to every in-process borrower (S-2)
- [ ] Pipeline review: stuck >7 days in a stage? Decide next action or pause
- [ ] Scrub new call lists against National DNC + internal DNC before any marketing calls
- [ ] Publish/approve 1 educational content piece (only after sponsor approval process)
- [ ] Update the "Leads missing consent note" count on the Summary tab to zero

## Monthly
- [ ] Sponsor/compliance: submit any new marketing pieces for approval; file approvals
- [ ] Review opt-out list processing (CAN-SPAM: honor within 10 business days)
- [ ] Partner review: who referred, who to thank (non-monetary, within company policy)
- [ ] Archive closed files per company record-retention policy

## Per new file

- [ ] Program fit checked against **current company/investor guidelines** (not this toolkit)

- [ ] Document checklist sent; tracker row created; consent logged
