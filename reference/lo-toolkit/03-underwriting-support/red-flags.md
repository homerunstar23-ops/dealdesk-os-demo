# Red-flag list (pre-submission review)

> **⚠️ Responsibility & approval notice.** Cory remains responsible for his licensing, sponsorship (sponsoring-broker approval of all marketing and outside business activities), RESPA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, fair lending (ECOA/FHA/UDAAP) and state rules. **Get sponsoring-broker compliance sign-off before any public or borrower-facing use.** This is educational/support material only — not legal advice, not a credit decision, and not a replacement for underwriters, compliance staff or company systems. Keep borrower data (NPI) only in company-approved systems. Fill every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` before use.

Support checklist to escalate questions **to underwriting/compliance** — not a decision tool. Flagging something does not mean wrongdoing; follow company procedures (including any fraud-reporting procedures). Never coach a borrower to hide or alter information.

## Occupancy / purpose
- Borrower or family member may live in the "investment" property; primary-residence signals (homestead filing, driver's license address, mail)
- Cash-out proceeds intended for personal/household use (affects business-purpose determination)

## Transaction
- Non-arm's-length deal (related parties, seller = relative/partner) not disclosed
- Rapid resale / flip with large unexplained price increase; multiple assignments; inflated assignment fee
- Contract changes late in the process (price, credits, parties)
- Seller concessions or repairs credits not reflected in docs; side agreements

## Value / income
- ARV unsupported by comps; appraiser pressure requests (never allowed — appraisal independence)
- Rents far above market; leases between related parties; STR income without history
- Rehab budget inconsistent with scope; contractor is borrower's relative without disclosure

## Funds / identity
- Large or unusual deposits without clear source; funds from unknown third parties
- Mismatched names, addresses, SSN issues or documents that look altered
- Entity recently formed with unclear members; straw-buyer indicators
- Pressure to close unusually fast while avoiding standard steps

## Communications
- Requests to change wire instructions by email; anyone asking to bypass the title company
- Borrower asks you to "word" documents to qualify — stop and escalate
