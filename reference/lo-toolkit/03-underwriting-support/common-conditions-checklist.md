Conditions commonly seen on business-purpose investor loans. Actual conditions come only from underwriting. Use this to prepare borrowers and pre-empt delays.

## Borrower / entity
- [ ] Updated asset statements (all pages); explanations for large deposits; proof of funds to close
- [ ] Entity: operating agreement showing members/authority, good standing, EIN, signer resolution
- [ ] Guarantor ID; track-record verification (settlement statements, deeds)
- [ ] Letter of explanation for credit items (as requested)

## Property / valuation
- [ ] Appraisal received and reviewed; rent schedule (DSCR); as-is and ARV (rehab)
- [ ] Insurance binder: correct mortgagee clause, coverage amount, loss-payee, effective dates; flood policy if required
- [ ] Title commitment: vesting matches, liens/judgments addressed, payoff letters
- [ ] Leases / rent roll / estoppels (as applicable)
- [ ] HOA questionnaire / budget (condos)

## Rehab / construction
- [ ] Final SOW & budget accepted; contractor license and insurance
- [ ] Permits/plans (ground-up); feasibility review if required
- [ ] Draw schedule acknowledgment

## Closing
- [ ] Final settlement statement review; cash to close verified
- [ ] Wire instructions verified by phone with title at a known number
- [ ] Business-purpose / occupancy certifications as required by the company
