# Referral-partner outreach: agents, wholesalers, property managers, contractors

> **⚠️ Responsibility & approval notice.** Cory remains responsible for his licensing, sponsorship (sponsoring-broker approval of all marketing and outside business activities), RESPA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, fair lending (ECOA/FHA/UDAAP) and state rules. **Get sponsoring-broker compliance sign-off before any public or borrower-facing use.** This is educational/support material only — not legal advice, not a credit decision, and not a replacement for underwriters, compliance staff or company systems. Keep borrower data (NPI) only in company-approved systems. Fill every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` before use.

> **RESPA Section 8 / referral-fee caution:** Do **not** offer, pay, split or accept fees, gifts, co-marketing payments, free services or anything of value in exchange for referrals. Co-marketing or MSAs require written compliance approval and fair-market-value, actual-services documentation. Business-purpose loans are generally exempt from RESPA, but company policy and state law can still prohibit this. Default: **value = education and service, never payment.**

## Positioning (what you offer partners)
- Fast, clear **scenario answers** for their investor clients (educational, no terms before application)
- Free **educational tools**: DSCR/flip/BRRRR calculators, deal package checklist (white-label only with sponsor approval)
- **Responsiveness**: status updates (borrower-authorized) so their deals close on time
- **Education sessions** (e.g., a lunch-and-learn on investor financing basics) — approved by compliance; no thing of value contingent on referrals

## Wholesalers
> "When your end buyers ask about financing on assignments, I can explain how short-term lenders commonly size loans (loan-to-cost vs. ARV) and what docs they'll need — including on assignment contracts. Want a one-page checklist to send buyers?"
*Note: wholesale/assignment deals can raise program-eligibility and disclosure questions — check guidelines; never help disguise assignment fees.*

## Property managers
> "Your owners who want to refinance or buy more doors often ask how rent affects loan size. I can do a free educational session for your owners on DSCR basics, and I'm a resource when they have a specific property."

## Contractors / GCs
> "Investors often stall because their scope of work isn't lender-ready. I share a free scope-of-work and draw-schedule template — would that help your investor clients?"

## Referral ask to past clients (no incentive)
> "If you know another investor who'd find the calculators or checklist useful, feel free to share {{SITE_LINK}} or my contact. I appreciate it."

## Partner tracking
Track partners in the pipeline tracker "Source" column. Never track "referrals owed" or anything that looks like a quid pro quo.
