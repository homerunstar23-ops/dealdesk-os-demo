# Consent & disclosure rules for every outbound template (read first)

> **⚠️ Responsibility & approval notice.** Cory remains responsible for his licensing, sponsorship (sponsoring-broker approval of all marketing and outside business activities), RESPA, TCPA, CAN-SPAM, fair lending (ECOA/FHA/UDAAP) and state rules. **Get sponsoring-broker compliance sign-off before any public or borrower-facing use.** This is educational/support material only — not legal advice, not a credit decision, and not a replacement for underwriters, compliance staff or company systems. Keep borrower data (NPI) only in company-approved systems. Fill every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` before use.

This page applies to **every** template in this folder. It is an educational summary, not legal advice; your sponsoring broker's compliance policy and current law control. Rules (especially FCC/TCPA consent rules) change — confirm with compliance before launching any campaign.

## 1. Required identity/disclosure block (placeholders)
Every outbound email, DM bio/profile, social post, landing page and printed piece that markets mortgage services should carry what your state(s) and sponsor require. Typical elements:
```
{{LO_NAME}}, {{TITLE}} | NMLS #{{NMLS_ID}}
{{SPONSOR_COMPANY_LEGAL_NAME}} | Company NMLS #{{COMPANY_NMLS_ID}}
{{STATE_LICENSE_DISCLOSURES — e.g., state license numbers / required licensing statements per state advertised in}}
{{EQUAL_HOUSING_OPPORTUNITY_STATEMENT_OR_LOGO — if required for the product/channel}}
{{COMPANY_ADDRESS}} | {{PHONE}} | www.nmlsconsumeraccess.org
```
- Advertising disclosure requirements are set largely by **state law** and your sponsor; they vary by state. Check each state where you'll advertise (NMLS State Licensing Requirements: https://mortgage.nationwidelicensingsystem.org/slr/Pages/default.aspx).
- Federal Reg Z requires the loan originator organization and individual LO name and NMLSR ID on certain **consumer** dwelling-secured loan documents (12 CFR 1026.36(g): https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/36/). Advertising rules for consumer credit: 12 CFR 1026.24 (https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/24/).
- Advertise only through your sponsor and under their name if their policy requires; outside "personal brands" generally need written outside-business-activity approval.

## 2. Email (CAN-SPAM) — every commercial email
- Accurate From / Reply-To / routing; honest subject line
- Identify as an advertisement where applicable
- Valid **physical postal address**
- Clear **opt-out**; process opt-outs for at least 30 days after sending; honor within **10 business days**; don't sell/transfer opted-out addresses
- You're responsible for vendors sending on your behalf
- Source: 16 CFR Part 316 (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-316)
- **Template footer:** `You're receiving this because {{HOW_OPTED_IN}}. Reply "unsubscribe" or click {{UNSUBSCRIBE_LINK}} to stop. {{PHYSICAL_ADDRESS}}`

## 3. Calls & texts (TCPA / Telemarketing / DNC)
- **Texts and calls using an autodialer or artificial/prerecorded (including AI-generated) voice to cell phones** for marketing generally require **prior express written consent**. Get consent in writing (web form with clear disclosure, checkbox not pre-checked, not a condition of service), and store the record (date, time, source, wording, phone number).
- **Manual 1:1 marketing calls:** scrub against the **National Do Not Call Registry** (https://telemarketing.donotcall.gov/) and your internal DNC list unless an exemption (e.g., established business relationship, written permission) applies — confirm with compliance.
- Respect calling-time limits (federal rule: no telemarketing calls before 8 a.m. or after 9 p.m. recipient's local time — 47 CFR 64.1200: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-64/subpart-L/section-64.1200). **Some states (including Florida) have stricter "mini-TCPA" rules on hours, frequency and consent** — have compliance confirm.
- Every marketing text: identify yourself/company and include opt-out (`Reply STOP to opt out`). Honor opt-outs promptly across all channels.
- No purchased "aged lead" lists or shared-consent lead lists without compliance review — consent often doesn't transfer.
- FCC overview: https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/telephone-consumer-protection-act

## 4. LinkedIn / social DMs
- Platform terms apply (no automation tools that violate them).
- Profile must carry required NMLS/company disclosures if it markets lending services; posts may be "advertising" requiring sponsor pre-approval.
- Don't move a DM conversation to text/calls without obtaining the right consent.

## 5. Referral partners (RESPA Section 8)
- **Never pay or give anything of value for referrals** (cash, gift cards, paid dinners tied to referrals, splitting fees, "marketing fees" that are really referral payments) when RESPA applies. Business-purpose loans are generally exempt from RESPA (12 CFR 1024.5(b)(2)), but state law, company policy and other rules may still restrict this — **treat all referral compensation as prohibited unless compliance approves in writing.** See `../04-compliance-reference/respa-section-8.md`.

## 6. Fair lending in marketing
- Target by **business criteria** (investors, property types, deal stages) — never by race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, familial status, disability, receipt of public assistance or other protected characteristics. No "ideal for young professionals"-style language. Ad targeting on platforms must not exclude protected groups or neighborhoods. See `../04-compliance-reference/fair-lending-advertising.md`.

## 7. Content claims (UDAAP)
- No "guaranteed approval," "you qualify," "lowest rates," "no credit check" claims; no specific rate/payment without required disclosures and sponsor approval; no invented statistics or testimonials.
