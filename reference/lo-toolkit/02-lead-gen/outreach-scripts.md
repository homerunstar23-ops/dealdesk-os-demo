## 1. Call scripts (manual dial; consented or DNC-scrubbed)

### 1a. Inbound lead call-back (tool download / web form)
> "Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, this is {{LO_NAME}}, a licensed loan officer with {{COMPANY_NAME}}. You {{downloaded the deal checklist / used the DSCR calculator}} — is now an OK time for two minutes?
> [Yes] Great. Are you looking at a specific property, or getting ready for the next one?
> [Discovery] What's the strategy — rental, flip, BRRRR, ground-up? Rough price and timeline? Have you done similar deals before?
> [Value] Based on that, I'd normally look at {{DSCR / loan-to-cost / ARV}}. I can't quote terms until we take an application, but I can walk you through what lenders typically look at so there are no surprises.
> [Next step] Want me to email a checklist for this deal type and set a 15-minute call once you have numbers?
> [Close] Thanks — I'll send it now. If you ever want me to stop calling, just say so."

### 1b. Past client check-in
> "Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, {{LO_NAME}} with {{COMPANY_NAME}}. Checking in since we closed {{PROPERTY_SHORT}} — how's it performing? Anything on your radar for the next deal? … If someone in your network is looking at investor financing, I'm always happy to be a resource."
*(No reward/gift for referrals — see RESPA note.)*

### 1c. Voicemail (manual, never prerecorded)
> "Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, {{LO_NAME}}, licensed loan officer with {{COMPANY_NAME}}, NMLS {{NMLS_ID}}. Following up on {{REASON}}. I'll also send a quick email. My number is {{PHONE}}. If you'd prefer I not call, just let me know."

## 2. Email scripts (CAN-SPAM footer + disclosure block required)

If you'd like, reply with the property type, city and your timeline and I'll send the checklist lenders commonly use for that kind of deal, plus a few questions to ask any lender. Educational only — terms are only available after a full application.

Subject: Still looking for your next deal?
> Hi {{FIRST_NAME}}, quick check-in. If you're analyzing anything, the free {{TOOL_LINK}} might help. When you have something under contract, I'm happy to walk through it. {{DISCLOSURE_BLOCK}} {{CAN_SPAM_FOOTER}}

## 3. LinkedIn / DM scripts (profile must carry NMLS + company disclosures)

### 3a. Connection note (≤300 chars)
> "Hi {{FIRST_NAME}} — I'm a licensed loan officer working with real estate investors in {{MARKET}}. I share educational posts on DSCR, flip and BRRRR financing math. Happy to connect."

### 3b. After they engage with a post
> "Thanks for the comment on {{POST_TOPIC}}. I put together a free deal-package checklist investors use before talking to lenders — want the link? No pitch."

### 3c. They ask about a deal
> "Happy to help. Easiest is a quick call or email so I can follow my company's process — what's the best email? I can't quote terms in DMs, but I can explain what lenders typically look at."
